One image that sums up the world's news of Tuesday, 17 November 2026: a
newspaper front page — "The World Today". Lead story: the Kerch Bridge
collapses in a Ukrainian strike, with Russian missiles hitting Kyiv's
bridges in return. Also: tankers hit by mines in the Strait of Hormuz as a
third US carrier arrives, Trump opens his Asia tour at the ASEAN summit in
Manila, Nvidia reports after the bell, a ULA Vulcan rocket fails and
destroys 40 Amazon satellites, and more.

The three photo panels are AI-rendered illustrations of the reported
events; all headlines and captions are drawn in crisp type so the page
reads at any size.

## Sources

The headlines on the front page were drawn from the morning news
round-ups and wire coverage of Tuesday, 17 November 2026: the daily
early-edition briefing from Just Security and the daily brief from
Level Headlines, cross-checked against reporting from Reuters and the
BBC, plus the day's briefing from Zark News. The Kerch Bridge strike
and the retaliatory hits on Kyiv bridges, the mine strikes on tankers
in the Strait of Hormuz, the ASEAN summit in Manila, and the business
and technology items in the "Also Today" strip all come from that
morning's coverage; the three photo panels are AI-rendered
illustrations of the reported events, not photographs.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This copies `entry.png` (a 1024×1024 JPEG, 309 KB) to the output
directory as `entry.png`. No network, no dependencies beyond the Python
standard library; running it twice writes identical bytes.
